Case Study 01 / Residential

Upper East Side Townhouse

Reimagining a nineteenth-century stone townhouse through architectural volumetric clarity, plaster walls, and custom bronze millwork.

Location

Upper East Side, New York

Scale

7,200 sq ft / 5 Levels

Scope

Interior Architecture & Furniture

Completion

2025

The Context

Restoring structural poise and natural light.

The owners acquired this historic 1890 limestone townhouse following decades of compartmentalized renovations that restricted daylight and obscured its grand proportions.

Our challenge was to open up internal sightlines and unify five floors around a central architectural stair hall. We replaced heavy partition walls with generous plaster portals, allowing east-to-west daylight to filter deeply into the garden and parlor levels.

Every material intervention was selected for longevity. The result is a home that feels both historically rooted in Manhattan tradition and modern in its spatial quietude.

01. The Parlor Floor Living Room Honed Tivoli Travertine & Hand-Slaked Lime Plaster
Culinary Space

Monolithic Travertine Kitchen

The kitchen features a seven-foot monolithic island carved from honed Roman travertine with integrated brass shadow-gap detailing. Cabinets are finished in smoked European oak with concealed hardware.

Private Sanctuary

Primary Suite & Bath

Occupying the entire third level, the primary suite features custom acoustic headboard paneling, wool velvet drapery, and a freestanding stone soaking tub set against floor-to-ceiling honed limestone slabs.
