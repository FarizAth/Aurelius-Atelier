Capabilities & Scope

Comprehensive interior architecture and spatial curation.

We guide projects from initial architectural concept through construction supervision, custom millwork, material sourcing, and final art placement.

01

Residential Interior Architecture

Primary Residences & Townhouses

Full architectural spatial restructuring for townhouses, lofts, and estate residences. We examine wall placements, ceiling geometry, portal openings, staircases, and custom stone fireplaces to build a harmonious structural foundation.

  • Spatial re-configuration & 3D volumetric planning
  • Architectural ceiling, lighting & acoustic plans
  • Custom stone, plaster & architectural metal details
  • Coordination with structural engineers & expeditors
02

Full-Service Interior Design

Turnkey Furnishing & Styling

Complete spatial curation bringing warmth, texture, and rare objects into harmony. We select vintage twentieth-century gallery pieces, design custom upholstered furniture, and specify hand-loomed rugs and lighting.

  • Furniture layout & tactile mood direction
  • Procurement & international shipping logistics
  • Textile curation (linen, mohair, bouclé, raw silk)
  • Turnkey white-glove installation & placement
03

Historic Renovation & Restoration

Preservation & Modern Adaptation

Sensitive architectural restoration for landmarked Manhattan brownstones, cast-iron Soho structures, and historic country estates. We preserve original moldings and masonry while seamlessly integrating modern climate systems and lighting.

  • Landmarks Preservation Commission (LPC) approvals
  • Restoration of historic plasterwork, wood, and iron
  • Concealed HVAC & smart home technology integration
04

Boutique Hospitality Interiors

Hotels, Lounges & Member Spaces

Creating atmospheric, memorable guest experiences for luxury boutique hotels, private dining rooms, and member lounges. We design spaces that balance aesthetic intimacy with durable commercial performance.

  • Brand narrative translation through spatial design
  • Acoustic layering & specialized ambient lighting
  • Custom commercial bar & dining furniture joinery
05

Furniture & Spatial Joinery Design

Bespoke Cabinetry & Architectural Woodwork

Custom millwork drawings for kitchens, dressing rooms, paneled libraries, and floating vanity units. Crafted by skilled artisans in solid oak, walnut, travertine, and patinated bronze.

06

Art, Antique & Object Curation

Fine Art & Sculptural Objects

Acquiring fine contemporary art, mid-century Scandinavian ceramics, and rare antique vessels from galleries and auctions across New York, Paris, and Tokyo to give spaces soul and personal depth.

07

Material & Finish Direction

Quarry Selection & Tonal Schemes

Directing stone slab selection, lime plaster formulations, custom wood stains, and metal patination for client projects and collaborating architectural practices.
