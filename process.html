Methodology & Rigor

The Art of Execution

Our six-stage process provides artistic discipline, absolute budget transparency, and seamless execution from concept to final key handover.

01

Discovery & Spatial Analysis

Stage One / Exploration

We examine the physical structure, orientation, sun angles, acoustic characteristics, and historical context of your space. Equally important, we spend time understanding how you live, host guests, store personal belongings, and experience quiet moments.

Deliverables: Architectural spatial audit, lifestyle brief & preliminary scope documentation.

02

Architectural Direction & Concept

Stage Two / Vision

We establish the overall spatial concept, key room layouts, sightline axes, and material mood. We present physical stone slabs, wood samples, and hand sketches to define the artistic atmosphere before technical drawings commence.

Deliverables: Physical material tray, initial floor plans, and spatial mood concepts.

03

Design Development & Geometry

Stage Three / Technical Precision

Our studio drafts comprehensive CAD drawing packages including floor plans, reflective ceiling plans, electrical layouts, stone tile coursing, and custom millwork construction details.

Deliverables: Full architectural drawing set, lighting schedules & itemized budget matrices.

04

Material Sourcing & Custom Fabrication

Stage Four / Artisanal Craft

We personally inspect and reserve stone blocks, approve wood veneer flitches, and supervise custom furniture prototypes with our joiners and bronze fabricators in New York and Europe.

Deliverables: Approved shop drawings, prototype sign-offs & procurement tracking schedules.

05

Construction Oversight & Site Direction

Stage Five / Execution

Our team conducts regular site visits, reviews trade submittals, and resolves site conditions alongside general contractors to ensure drawing details are executed without compromise.

Deliverables: Site visit reports, trade coordination & quality control reviews.

06

Final Curation & White-Glove Installation

Stage Six / Completion

We manage the delivery of all custom furnishings, vintage gallery pieces, custom rugs, window treatments, and art placement, presenting a fully realized home ready for immediate living.

Deliverables: Turnkey spatial installation, art hangings & maintenance handbooks.
