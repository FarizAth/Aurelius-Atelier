About The Practice

Dedicated to proportion, material truth, and quiet permanence.

Aurelius Atelier is an interior design studio based in Soho, New York. We approach interior spaces through an architectural lens, shaping volume and texture into refined environments.

Our Ethos

Architecture before ornamentation.

We operate on the conviction that interior environments should be experienced as architectural compositions. Decor alone cannot rescue a room with flawed spatial proportions or artificial light.

Our work focuses on fundamental elements: spatial pacing, sightline alignment, volumetric clarity, shadow behavior, and raw material authenticity. Whether working on a multi-story historic Manhattan townhouse or a modern coastal sanctuary, we remove unnecessary noise until only serene, purposeful architecture remains.

We believe a home should feel like a sanctuary. It must support human living with quiet grace, offering acoustic comfort, visual warmth, and an intimate connection to natural light.

Material Sourcing

The tactile weight of honest materials.

Material selection is central to our artistic practice. We reject synthetic veneers, artificial composite stones, and superficial surface treatments in favor of natural materials that age with dignity.

We personally select marble and travertine blocks directly from quarries in Tivoli and Carrara. Our timber is sourced from sustainably managed European oak and walnut forests, air-dried and finished with organic oils rather than harsh plastics.

Every texture in an Aurelius Atelier space invites human touch. The tactile contrast between slaked lime plaster, cold stone, and warm hand-loomed textiles creates an atmosphere of unforced luxury.

Client Experience

A collaborative relationship built on trust and clarity.

Designing a home or boutique hospitality space is an intimate journey. We act as artistic stewards, listening carefully to how our clients live, gather, and rest before sketching a single line.

We maintain total financial transparency, clear schedule management, and hands-on site supervision. Our clients trust us to manage complex architectural alterations with composure and precision.
