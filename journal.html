The Role of Natural Stone in Contemporary Interiors

Stone is perhaps the most fundamental building material known to human civilization. In an age dominated by synthetic composites and disposable surface laminates, unpolished travertine, honed marble, and monolithic limestone offer something rare: tectonic weight and permanent truth.

When specified with restraint, stone does not overpower a space. Instead, it grounds the room, absorbing and reflecting natural light in ways artificial materials cannot replicate.
